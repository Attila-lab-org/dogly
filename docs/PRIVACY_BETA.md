# Privacy Policy — Dogly Closed Beta

Last updated: staging beta.

## What we collect

- Account identifiers from Supabase Auth (email / Google subject)
- Dog profile fields you enter
- Behavior videos you upload (private Storage; server-generated paths only)
- Analysis outputs, feedback, usage ledger

## Retention

Raw behavior video is temporary. TTL starts at analysis completion (`RAW_MEDIA_TTL_HOURS`, default 24h), then deletion is scheduled and audited. The photo album never exposes raw behavior video.

## Your controls

From Settings → Privacy: export your data, delete your account, manage consents.
Research/training opt-in is OFF by default.
